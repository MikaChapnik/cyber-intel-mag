Cybersecurity Conferences

The major annual events for security professionals, researchers, and executives — from technical hacking conferences to strategic summits.

Dates shown are typical. Always verify current dates and locations on official conference websites before making travel plans.

RSA Conference

San Francisco, CA April / May

The world's largest cybersecurity conference, drawing 40,000+ attendees for keynotes, technical sessions, and the massive RSAC Expo featuring hundreds of vendors. Covers the full spectrum of enterprise security, policy, and emerging technology.

Official Website →

Black Hat USA

Las Vegas, NV August

The premier technical security event featuring cutting-edge research, in-depth training, and the infamous Briefings presentations by elite researchers. Black Hat is where zero-day disclosures, tool releases, and groundbreaking vulnerability research are unveiled.

Official Website →

DEF CON

Las Vegas, NV August (follows Black Hat)

One of the world's largest and most renowned hacker conventions, featuring CTF competitions, villages for specialized topics, and talks across offensive and defensive security. Known for its open, community-driven culture and deep technical content.

Official Website →

Black Hat Europe

London, UK December

The European edition of Black Hat brings elite security research and deep technical training to London. It serves as a key gathering for European security professionals and global researchers covering offensive techniques, malware analysis, and defensive innovation.

Official Website →

CyberUK

United Kingdom (city rotates) May

The UK government's flagship cybersecurity event, organized by the National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC). Focuses on national cyber strategy, government and industry collaboration, and the latest threat intelligence affecting UK organizations.

Official Website →

Infosecurity Europe

London, UK June

Europe's number one information security event, bringing together security professionals, practitioners, and vendors at ExCeL London. Strong on practical content for security managers, compliance professionals, and enterprise buyers.

Official Website →

SANS Summits

Various cities & virtual Year-round

SANS Institute runs a series of focused summits throughout the year on topics including threat hunting, cloud security, ICS/SCADA, and cyber defense. Known for deeply technical, practitioner-focused content delivered by top instructors.

Official Website →

BSides Events

Global (100+ cities) Year-round

Security BSides events are community-organized conferences held in cities worldwide, offering accessible grassroots security content and networking. BSides Las Vegas, London, and San Francisco are among the largest and most prominent.

Official Website →

mWISE (Mandiant/Google)

Washington, D.C. September

mWISE (Mandiant Worldwide Information Security Exchange) is Google Cloud's practitioner-focused security conference emphasizing threat intelligence, frontline incident response, and nation-state threats. Known for frank, unfiltered analysis from Mandiant researchers.

Official Website →

Gartner Security & Risk Management Summit

National Harbor, MD & London June

Gartner's premier security event for CISOs and senior security executives focusing on strategic frameworks, vendor evaluations, and Magic Quadrant insights. A key venue for decision-makers evaluating security investments and organizational strategy.

Official Website →

Identity Week

London, UK & Washington, D.C. June

A specialist conference covering digital identity, authentication, biometrics, and access management. Brings together government, enterprise, and technology leaders to discuss the evolving identity landscape as IAM becomes central to zero trust strategies.

Official Website →
